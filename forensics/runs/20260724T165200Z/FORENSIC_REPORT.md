## 8. Verdict — what to rewrite / remove / formatting-only

1. Engines that improve lessons: **LCE_ADAPTATIONS** (+77 ECS; only stage that lifts learner score 38.5 → 61.8).
2. Engines that damage lessons:
   - **PQLE** (−17 ECS): measurable learner-score drop 61.5 → 58.1; shrinks mainstream/teacher/visual text (standard 6942→5538 chars) without improving clarity.
   - **DIAGRAMS enricher** (−10 ECS): injects repeated boilerplate (“Quick Revision” / “Think About It”) across nearly every adaptation; repetition rises.
3. Engines that contribute nothing to learner surface in this run: **ULI, SIF, UVIE, LCE_CLG_BOARD, VOCABULARY (post-author), HTML_RENDER** (score flat). Note: ULI/SIF/UVIE feed LCE inputs but do not change learner text until adaptations author; their ECS≈0 means no *direct* learner delta in this harness.
4. Stages that should be rewritten:
   - **PQLE** (rewrite or demote to formatting-only; it currently reduces quality).
   - **PMES** (ECS +9 masks catastrophic content): blame excerpts show template pollution — e.g. “Have you ever wondered why **opening** matters…”, “Think of **opening** like a…”, “New word focus: **opening**” on a Water Cycle lesson. That is not curriculum-grounded teaching; it is broken master-teacher templating. **Rewrite or hard-gate before any further polish layers.**
   - **PEEC/EPP/content fidelity** bundle: net ECS −8; expands text, leaks “exit ticket” / “in this lesson”, does not raise learner score.
5. Stages that should be removed or hard-gated next (evidence):
   - Stacked polish after LCE authorship (PQLE→PMES→PEEC→EPP→fidelity) — disable probes show final score returns to ~61.5 whether polish runs or not, while blame shows polish *mutates* wording badly (PMES “opening”). Net: risk without learner gain.
   - Adaptation clones failing ≥60% identity: **ld↔dyslexia (0.88)**, **visual↔teacher (0.94)**, **auditory↔teacher (0.72)**, **ld↔adhd**, **visual↔auditory**, **adhd↔dyslexia**.
6. Stages that should become formatting-only: **HTML_RENDER**, and treat **SIF/UVIE** as optional enrichers until they prove learner-text gains.
