# Biological Process Guide

`engines/biology_intelligence/processes.py` supplies **process metadata** only.

## Process families

- Cell processes (mitosis, meiosis, membrane transport)
- Plant processes (photosynthesis, respiration, transpiration, life cycles)
- Physiology (cellular respiration, digestion, gas exchange, homeostasis)
- Genetics (inheritance, protein synthesis, DNA replication)
- Ecology (food-web interactions, energy flow, nutrient cycles)
- Evolution (natural selection, evolutionary relationships)

## Teaching lenses

Systems thinking · structure–function · cause–effect · concept mapping
