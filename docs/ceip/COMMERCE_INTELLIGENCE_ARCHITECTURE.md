# Commerce Intelligence Architecture

**Pack:** Commerce & Economics Intelligence Pack (CEIP)  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Smoke:** `COMMERCE_ECONOMICS_INTELLIGENCE_SMOKE_OK`

## Role

CEIP is Alora AI’s commerce Subject Intelligence Pack for accounting, economics, business studies, finance, entrepreneurship, marketing, management, taxation, and financial literacy. Built on **SIF + SICS**. Enriches verified ULI without inventing curriculum.

## Subject family registration

One analysis implementation registers over:

`commerce`, `economics`, `business_studies`

School-level economics markers inside Social Science lessons remain enrichable via SSIP’s `social_science` pack; the `economics` subject key is owned by CEIP.

- No invented journal answers, market numbers, tax advice, or business plans
